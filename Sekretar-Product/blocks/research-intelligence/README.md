# Research Intelligence Block

Research Intelligence prepares external research for confirmed tasks.

It is not ordinary chat and not just search over old meetings. Internal memory and meetings may provide context, but the main purpose is external intelligence gathering.

Responsibilities:

- deciding whether a task needs external research;
- creating research jobs;
- collecting minimal external information;
- preparing short research briefings;
- asking the user before deeper research;
- recording sources and confidence.

Research must not perform external actions on behalf of the user without confirmation.
